The problem

Learning shouldn't stop at the answer.

Traditional digital learning can tell us whether an answer is right or wrong, but often misses the reasoning behind it.

Passive consumption

Students watch, read and consume information without always actively processing it.

Answer-focused

Most systems measure the final answer instead of understanding the student's reasoning.

Hidden gaps

A student can get an answer right while still having an incomplete mental model.

The killer feature

What if you had to teach the AI?

Instead of AI immediately giving students an answer, MindLab creates a conversation where the student has to explain, reason and correct the AI Student.

AI Student intentionally has believable misconceptions.
Follow-up questions challenge the student's reasoning.
Explanations become signals about conceptual understanding.
Students can type or use their voice.
I think Binary Search works on any array because we keep dividing it in half.
Actually, it needs sorted data because...
But why does sorted order let you discard the entire other half?
MindLab is learning about the learner. The conversation reveals more than a simple right-or-wrong answer.
