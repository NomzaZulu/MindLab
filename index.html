The problem

Most digital learning still rewards remembering answers.

Students can watch explanations, complete quizzes and receive scores without ever being asked to reveal how they actually reasoned.

Consume

Watch videos, read explanations and move through content without necessarily engaging deeply with the concept.

Answer

Traditional assessments mostly tell us whether the final answer was right or wrong.

But why?

The reasoning behind an incorrect answer — and the misconception causing it — often remains invisible.

Our learning loop

Make the learner part of the process.
