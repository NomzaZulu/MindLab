🧠 Learning through thinking
The problem

More content doesn't always mean more learning.

Digital education has made information easier to access. But students can still remain passive — watching, reading and getting answers without deeply reasoning through a concept.

Consume

Students watch lectures, read notes and move from one piece of content to the next.

Ask AI

AI can produce an answer instantly — sometimes before the student has had a chance to think.

Miss the “why”

Systems often know what answer was wrong, but not the reasoning behind the mistake.

Learning modes

Three ways to actively learn a concept.

Different concepts need different experiences. MindLab provides multiple interaction styles instead of one fixed lesson format.

Teach · AI Student

Explain a concept to an AI student that asks questions, makes believable mistakes and forces you to reason clearly.

Discover · Knowledge Detective

Experiment · Experiment Lab

The killer feature

What if students taught the AI?

Instead of an AI tutor continuously explaining answers, MindLab creates a confused “student” that the learner has to teach.

As the student explains, MindLab can analyse the explanation and interaction for signs of understanding, uncertainty and probable conceptual gaps.

Student has to retrieve and explain the concept.
AI asks targeted follow-up questions instead of giving away the answer.
Responses become signals for the learner model.
I understand that Binary Search divides the array in half. So it should work even if the array isn't sorted, right?
No, because if it's sorted we know which half cannot contain the value.
But why can you be sure that the discarded half doesn't contain the value?
